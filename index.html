Ocean County, New Jersey

Ocean County is the largest county in New Jersey by total area. Tourism continues to grow as people settle south and Long Beach Island draws more people to the shore. The current total population is 646,434 people. Fun fact: During the 19th century, Ocean County is a central hub for cranberry production.
