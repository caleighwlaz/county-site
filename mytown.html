My Favorite Places in Manahawkin!

My hometown is Manahawkin, New Jersey. There are tons of places to hang out, and there's great food!

Not Just Yogurt

Not Just Yogurt is a froyo place that I love. I go there often when I am home from breaks.

Manahawkin Lake Park

Manahawkin Lake Park is a local park near the froyo place above. I love to walk around and watch Manahawkin Lake.

Element

Element is my favorite place to get sushi. My family often gets it to celebrate.

Fusaro's

Fusaro's is a local pizzeria that makes one of the best pizzas. I miss their pizzas when I'm at school.
